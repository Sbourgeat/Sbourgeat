# 💫 About Me:
Greetings, I’m Samuel👋🏼

I’m a PhD in Computational Neuroscience working as an AI engineer at EPFL, and I love to experiment with cybersecurity and ethical hacking.

From the very beginning of my academic journey, I was driven by the desire to understand complex systems such as the human brain. The rapid emergence of artificial intelligence as a transformative force in society sparked my curiosity and motivated me to explore both its underlying principles and its responsible use. Today, I work at the intersection of AI and higher education, developing tools that help students and educators harness these technologies effectively and ethically.
